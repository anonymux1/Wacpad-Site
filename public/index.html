Frequently Asked Questions

How does license activation work?

After completing checkout, you will immediately receive your WP1-... license key on screen and in your email. Simply open WacPad Desktop on your PC or Mac, click Settings → License Status, paste the key, and click Activate. Your connected iPad will instantly unlock the full canvas and remove all session limits.

Does it require an active internet connection to draw?

No! The WacPad Pro license key uses embedded HMAC-SHA256 cryptographic signatures that validate 100% offline. You never need an internet connection to use WacPad once activated.

Can I use my Pro license on both Windows and Mac?

Yes. Your single lifetime license key can be activated on your desktop workstations across Windows 10/11 and macOS.
