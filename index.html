Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

WacPad is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled across the WacPad iOS application and desktop companion hosts:

  • Drawing & Input Data: All Apple Pencil strokes, pressure dynamics, touch coordinates, and hotkey actions are transmitted exclusively over your private local connection (Direct USB cable or local Wi-Fi). No drawing or keystroke data is ever uploaded to external cloud servers.
  • Advertising: The free tier of the iOS application may display mobile advertisements provided by ad partners (such as AppLovin MAX). These third-party networks may collect contextual device data (e.g., device model, OS version, IP address, and advertising identifiers if permitted) to deliver and measure ads in compliance with applicable privacy regulations.
  • Pro Licensing: When activating WacPad Pro, your purchase email and license status are validated via cryptographic verification. We do not store or process payment card details; payments are handled directly by secure payment processors (such as Stripe).
